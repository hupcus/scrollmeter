Races: the pipeline's flushes and the erase share `MeasurementMonitor.writeLock`; the erase bumps `dataEpoch` under it, and the pipeline drops everything it accumulated before the bump (accumulator and open sessions) instead of writing it back. The usage sync is paused for the erase (`UsageSyncer.exclusive`) and a **data floor** (erase time) is stored: the sync never re-imports time in app from before it, even though the usage stats still hold it.
